## Advanced Strategies for Surviving the Encounter

To consistently clear the most demanding versions of this battle, competitive players employ high-level evasion techniques originally developed for super-bosses like Jevil and Sans.

### 1. Master the Micro-Tap

Holding down movement keys sends your SOUL drifting rapidly toward arena walls, where hitboxes frequently cluster. Instead of holding directional keys, train yourself to "micro-tap." Tapping your directional key moves the SOUL a few pixels at a time, allowing you to slip through tight gaps between incoming slashes.

### 2. Utilize Hitbox Leniency (Invulnerability Frames)

When your SOUL takes damage, it flashes white for roughly 1 to 1.5 seconds. During these Invulnerability Frames (i-frames), you are immune to all incoming hitboxes. If you find yourself trapped in an inescapable crosshatch attack, intentionally brush against a low-damage projectile to activate i-frames, then pass safely through a high-damage lethal strike.

### 3. Screen Edge Management

Avoid getting pinned in the absolute corners of the bullet box. Boss attacks are designed to ricochet off corners, effectively doubling the density of projectiles in those areas. Maintain a defensive posture in the lower-middle third of the box, giving yourself escape routes in all directions.

### 4. Audio-Visual Synchronization

Many community creators synchronize Kris's strike frames with musical downbeats. Listening carefully to the background track often alerts you to incoming blade flashes a split-second before the visual telegraph renders.
