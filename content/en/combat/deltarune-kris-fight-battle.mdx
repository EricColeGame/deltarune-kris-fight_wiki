## Advanced Combat Tactics: Graze, TP Management, and Action Prioritization

Resource flow dictates the pace of every encounter. Tension Points represent the party's shared energy pool, powering devastating spells like Susie's Rude Buster and essential recovery spells like Ralsei's Heal Prayer. When engaging in a **deltarune kris fight battle**, the player must actively harvest TP rather than relying passively on defensive stances.

Grazing is the most efficient way to build Tension Points. By navigating Kris's red SOUL within pixels of incoming enemy projectiles, you trigger a graze audio cue and visual spark. Each grazed bullet feeds your TP bar. High-risk grazing allows skilled players to fire off powerful spells on consecutive turns, shortening grueling combat sequences drastically.

```
Graze Proximity -> Rapid TP Generation -> High-Tier ACT / Rude Buster -> Fast Battle Resolution
```

To maintain an optimal tempo, prioritize actions based on enemy intent. If a foe displays low mercy tolerance, spend Kris's turn executing specific ACTs that increase mercy percentages rapidly. If dealing with lethal projectile waves, have Kris defend while Susie executes S-Actions or consumes restorative items.

Mastering the rhythm between aggressive grazing and defensive staging ensures your team enters every turn with ample resources. Kris's zero-cost ACT commands allow you to save your hard-earned TP for critical clutch heals or high-impact finishing moves.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes Kris's role unique in combat compared to Susie and Ralsei?
Kris is the only party member who can perform standard ACT commands without spending Tension Points (TP). While Susie focuses on high physical damage and Ralsei specializes in healing and support spells, Kris coordinates team actions, manipulates enemy mercy meters, and serves as the physical vessel for the player's SOUL.

### How do I maintain full party health in a Deltarune Kris fight battle?
To keep your party healthy, maintain a high graze rate to generate consistent TP for Ralsei’s Heal Prayer or Dual Heal. Additionally, have Kris defend when boss patterns turn chaotic to cut incoming damage in half, and keep multi-target restorative items like the Top Cake or Spin Cake in your inventory.

### Can Kris use offensive magic spells during combat?
In standard combat, Kris has a base Magic stat of zero and cannot cast independent offensive spells. However, Kris can unlock and command special collaborative team techniques (such as X-Slash or S-Action) that utilize TP to deal massive burst damage or bypass enemy defenses.

### Why do some fan games feature a boss battle against Kris?
Because the overarching story highlights a separation between Kris’s own will and the player-controlled SOUL, community creators love exploring scenarios where the party faces Kris directly. These fan projects explore Kris's potential abilities as an autonomous adversary, featuring rapid sword attacks and challenging projectile patterns.
