## Phase-by-Phase Survival: Attack Patterns and Dodge Windows

Boss battles centered on Kris often divide the engagement into escalating phases. As the encounter progresses, attack animations blend together, reducing reaction windows and demanding proactive rather than reactive positioning.

### Phase 1: Spatial Slashes and Light Blades

The opening phase focuses on testing basic spatial awareness. Kris’s attacks generally start with linear blade projections that cut across the combat box in cardinal and diagonal directions. Before a slash appears, faint indicator lines or audio cues typically give you roughly 18 to 24 frames of warning.

*   **Linear Thrust:** A piercing blade moves directly down the center axis. Maintain a position roughly one-third of the distance from either horizontal wall to bypass this without moving.
*   **Cross Slash:** Two perpendicular blades sweep simultaneously. Settle into the lower-left or lower-right quadrant, as the intersection points at the center carry extended lingering hitboxes.
*   **Homing Daggers:** Small projectile knives spawn around the perimeter and converge on your Soul's location. Lead these projectiles in a gentle circular path along the perimeter of the box rather than darting erratically across the center.
