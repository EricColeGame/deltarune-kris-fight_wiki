## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the core premise of the deltarune kris fight theory?
The core premise of the **deltarune kris fight theory** is that the red SOUL controlled by the player and Kris's true persona are separate entities with conflicting desires. As Kris increasingly resists player commands and acts autonomously during night sequences, this tension will eventually culminate in a direct, playable boss fight between the player and Kris.

### Can Kris survive without the player's SOUL?
In-game cutscenes at the ends of Chapters 1 and 2 demonstrate that Kris can survive without the SOUL inside their body, but only for limited durations. Their physical movements become sluggish, unsteady, and visibly taxing, implying that while the SOUL is not their personal essence, it acts as a vital power source required for sustained life.

### Who would the player control if Kris is the boss?
The two most prominent theories suggest that the player would either command Susie and Ralsei (the remaining Lightner and Darkner heroes), or the player's SOUL will be reunited with the discarded vessel constructed at the very beginning of Chapter 1, pitting our chosen creation directly against Kris.

### How does the Snowgrave Route impact the likelihood of a Kris fight?
The Snowgrave (or Weird) Route significantly increases the narrative likelihood of a violent confrontation. By forcing Kris to manipulate and traumatize Noelle, the player acts as a purely tyrannical force, giving Kris every moral and personal motivation to eradicate the player's control by any means necessary.
