In this outcome, the player chooses never to strike Kris with a weapon. Instead, you spend each turn observing their micro-movements, calling out shared memories, or offering familiar items like the Hot Chocolate or Broken Sword. 

To achieve this finale, you must execute the "Reminisce" and "Release Grip" ACT commands during turns where Kris prepares their signature sweeping blade attack. Choosing pure aggression yields the darkest resolution available in custom encounters. If you continually target Kris with maximum damage strikes, their combat stance shifts from measured strikes to erratic, desperate slashes.

### 3. The Severance Ending (Neutral Route)
The neutral path occurs when players alternate haphazardly between fighting and talking. While Kris survives the encounter, the relationship between the vessel and the controlling entity becomes permanently strained. 

Kris retreats into the shadows without speaking, refusing to collect their dropped equipment. This outcome offers bittersweet closure: neither party is destroyed, yet the fundamental trust required to seal future Dark Fountains is irrevocably fractured.

### Tactical Survival Tips
- **Optimize Your Inventory:** Stock at least three Revive Mints and two Club Sandwiches before triggering the encounter.
- **Learn the "Graze" Windows:** Grazing Kris's sword slashes charges TP at nearly double the standard rate, allowing you to use high-cost ACTs like "Dual Heal" or "Peace Talk" earlier in the cycle.
- **Watch the Battle Box Borders:** In Phase 3, Kris cuts the battle box in half. Positioning your SOUL on the far-right quadrant gives you the widest reaction window against incoming projectile bursts.

Fan developers typically ignore the canonical single-ending premise in favor of classic branching systems inspired by *Undertale*.
