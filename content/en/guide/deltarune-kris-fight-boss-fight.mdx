- **Dodging Strategy:** Stay dead center during the initial tell, then immediately tap into the widest quadrant as the red telegraph lines solidify. Do not hold movement keys, as the momentum will carry your SOUL directly into the residual slash trails.

### 2. Dark Blade Flurry

- **Dodging Strategy:** Keep the SOUL grounded at the bottom third of the box. Circle around the bouncing projectiles rather than trying to squeeze between the falling blade outlines.

### 3. Fountain Sunder
Kris stabs the floor, spawning expanding shockwaves that ripple toward the edges of the box while summoning dark smoke that limits visibility.
- **Dodging Strategy:** Jump across the ground ripples with rhythmic, short hops. Treat this phase like a rhythm minigame; mistiming a jump will bounce your SOUL repeatedly, causing massive stacking damage.

## Pro-Tips for Bullet-Hell Dodging

Mastering the evasive maneuvers in any version of this duel demands sharp mechanical discipline:
- **Use Micro-Taps Over Holding Keys:** Press arrow or WASD keys in light, measured taps. Continuous holding causes your SOUL to gain maximum velocity quickly, making it difficult to stop before clipping a sword edge.
- **Watch the Center Point, Not the SOUL:** Fix your eyes on the spatial gaps between Kris’s incoming slashes rather than staring directly at your own red heart. Evasion is about spotting open safe zones early.
- **Exploit Invulnerability Frames (I-Frames):** If you take a hit from a low-damage peripheral projectile, immediately reposition through a high-damage slash zone while your temporary flicker invulnerability is active.
