## Kris Attack Patterns and How to Dodge Them

Dodge patterns in this encounter emphasize positioning over erratic dashing. Kris's bullet patterns reward players who utilize tiny micro-movements rather than frantic sweeping across the box. 

Following this dedicated **deltarune kris fight walkthrough**, examine the exact trajectories and warning indicators for each primary attack pattern below.

### 1. Linear Sword Arc (Horizontal & Vertical Cleave)
Kris raises their blade, causing thin warning beams to flash across the battle arena. After a 0.5-second delay, sharp slash trails slice across the grid.
* **Evasion Strategy:** Stay near the center of the box while the warning lines display. The moment the lines solidify, take a single tap up or down into the dead zone. Avoid hugging the edges of the board, as Kris frequently cascades secondary sparks along the borders.

### 2. The Apple & Knife Toss
A barrage of falling red projectiles drops from the ceiling, bouncing erratically as Kris launches fast horizontal knives across the bottom third of the screen.
* **Evasion Strategy:** Player experience shows that hovering in the upper-third quadrant avoids the knife sweeps entirely. Keep your eyes on the bouncing apples and slip between them as they apex.

### 3. SOUL Tether Trap
Kris uses Dark energy to momentarily tether the red SOUL to a central anchor point, creating an elastic pulling force while rotating blade hazards circle the perimeter.
* **Evasion Strategy:** Fight against the gravitational pull with steady, rhythmic counter-taps on your directional keys. Orbit clockwise along with the blade rotation rather than attempting to cut across the center.

## Advanced Tactics: Maximizing Tension Points (TP) and Grazing

To conquer the encounter with minimal consumable usage, you must master the art of intentional grazing. The graze mechanic rewards you with bonus TP every time an enemy projectile brushes your collision boundary without striking your inner hurtbox.

Community reports indicate that Kris's sword beams have surprisingly forgiving graze boxes along their trailing edges. Instead of fleeing to the far opposite side of the screen when a slash passes, position your SOUL just a few pixels behind the passing wave. This strategy can instantly yield 15% to 25% TP in a single turn.

### Key Grazing Tips
* **Equip Graze Enhancers:** Accessories that widen your graze perimeter allow you to generate TP while staying well outside lethal collision zones.
* **The Defend-Graze Multiplier:** When a party member Defends, their TP gain from nearby grazes is enhanced, allowing you to prep a high-cost spell in a single round.
* **Corner Trap Prevention:** Never allow Kris's moving slash walls to push you flush into the arena corners. Corners eliminate two directions of escape, drastically multiplying the chance of taking lethal consecutive hits.

By implementing the advanced tactics in this **deltarune kris fight walkthrough**, you transform defensive turns into offensive momentum, keeping your squad healthy while relentlessly draining Kris's resolve.
