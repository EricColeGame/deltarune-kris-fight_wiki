## Technical Guide: Extracting, Modifying, and Implementing Battle Sprites

For game developers and pixel artists interested in studying combat animations, understanding how sprites are structured within GameMaker Studio provides valuable practical knowledge. The original sprite assets are organized into multi-frame atlases that govern timing and offset parameters.

When importing or editing a **deltarune kris fight kris sprite** for community fangames or animation studies, maintain the following structural rules:

1.  **Strict 2x2 Pixel Scale:** *DELTARUNE* renders its sprites at an internal native resolution that scales up evenly. Mixing raw 1x1 pixels alongside 2x2 pixels creates visual dissonance known as "mixels." Always ensure your pixel grid matches the established engine scale.
2.  **Origin Alignment:** The character origin should sit at the bottom-center of the feet. Shifting this point across animation cycles causes Kris to "slide" across the battlefield floor between the idle loop and the attack windup.
3.  **Palette Consistency:** Use the official RGB values for Kris's Dark World palette: skin tone (`#5577AA` to `#6699CC`), armor plating (`#CCCCCC` with `#777788` shading), and cape fabric (`#EE4488` highlights down to `#771144` shadows).
4.  **Action Telegraphing:** In turn-based battle engines, telegraph frames must precede the main attack hit by at least 150 to 200 milliseconds to give the human eye time to register the coming blow.

Following these core principles guarantees that custom boss projects and animated shorts maintain the atmosphere and fluid visual rhythm established by the official releases.
