The trend originated from the end-of-chapter cinematics in *Deltarune*, where Kris removes the red SOUL from their body. Because the player controls the SOUL and not Kris's soul-less body, fans realized that a future confrontation between the two is narratively possible, sparking a wave of fan-made boss battles and animations.

### Where can I watch the best deltarune kris fight youtube fan animations and playthroughs?
You can find hundreds of community showcases by searching for custom battle concepts, Create Your Frisk playthroughs, and animated battle sequences directly on video streaming platforms. ### Are any of these Kris boss battles official canon?
No. All current encounters depicting a direct battle against Kris are fan-made projects, custom fangames, or animated concepts. While they draw heavily from official lore, dialogue, and sprites, Toby Fox and his development team have not officially released a battle against Kris in Chapters 1 or 2.

### Which game engines are most commonly used to make playable Kris fights?
Most playable fan encounters are built using Create Your Frisk (a Lua-based successor to Unitale), Godot Engine, custom Unity frameworks, or Roblox sandboxes. Each engine allows creators to simulate the distinct bullet-hell box and turn-based menu system of the original game.
